Booking terms

Effective 1 January 2026

1. Deposit and balance

A 20% deposit confirms your place. The balance is due 60 days before departure.

2. Cancellation by you

  • More than 60 days before departure: you lose the deposit.
  • 30–60 days: 50% of the total.
  • Less than 30 days: 100% of the total.

3. Changes by us

If we cancel a departure you get a full refund or a free transfer to another date.

4. Financial protection

All trips are protected under ATOL licence 11847.
