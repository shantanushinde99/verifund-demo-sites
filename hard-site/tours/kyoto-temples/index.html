⛩️ Kyoto Temples & Tea

7 days · max 12 travellers · ★ 4.9

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Arrive in Kyoto, welcome dinner in Gion
  2. Day 2: Fushimi Inari at sunrise, sake tasting
