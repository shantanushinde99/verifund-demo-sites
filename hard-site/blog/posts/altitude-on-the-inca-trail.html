Handling altitude on the Inca Trail

Dead Woman's Pass is 4,215 m high. Here's how to feel fine at the top.

Spend at least two nights in Cusco before you start, drink plenty of water and walk slowly — pole pole, as our guides say.

Warning signs

Headache, nausea or dizziness that gets worse means you should descend. Every guide carries oxygen.
