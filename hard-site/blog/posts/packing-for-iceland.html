What to pack for Iceland in winter

Layers, crampons and why you need a swimsuit in January.

Iceland's winter weather changes by the hour. Pack thermal base layers, a waterproof shell and microspikes for icy paths.

The checklist

  • Thermal base layers
  • Waterproof jacket and trousers
  • Microspikes or crampons
  • A swimsuit for the hot springs
